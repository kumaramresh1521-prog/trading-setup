📐 Authentic Excel Formulas & Drop-Down Guide

📊 Row & Column Formulas:
=A1 + 100 : Cell A1 me 100 jod kar Strike 2 banana
=C1 - D1 : Far Bid (Col C) - Near Ask (Col D)
=C1 - 2*D1 : 1:2 Ratio spread formula
=SUM(A1:A5) : 5 strikes ka sum
🧮 Mathematical Functions:
=ROUND(A1 * 1.01, 0) : 1% OTM rounded
=INT(A1 / 100) * 100 : Round to nearest hundred
=ABS(A1 - 23000) : Absolute distance
=AVERAGE(C1, D1) : Bid-Ask average
=IF(A1 > 23000, A1+100, A1+50) : Conditional strike
🟩 Excel Drop-Down / Autofill Handle (.jexcel_corner):
