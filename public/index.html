📌 How MTF (Margin Trading Facility) Data Works

Margin Trading Facility (MTF) allows investors to take leveraged positions in cash market equities by putting up a fraction of the capital (collateral), while the broker funds the rest. Under SEBI regulations, NSE and BSE publish mandatory EOD MTF disclosures with a 1-session reporting lag. Rising aggregate MTF book indicates building bullish retail/institutional leverage, while sharp drops signal margin liquidation and deleveraging cascades. Retail signals are detected using F&O stock concentration patterns; institutional signals are identified by large-cap non-F&O positions above ₹300 Cr.
